Your videos in 30+ languages, in your own voice

EasyVideoDub localizes long-form video for creators. We translate from your script, keep your voice and your music, and deliver audio tracks ready for multi-language publishing.

Talk to us about your channel

What you get

Your voice

Dubbing in the presenter's own voice, cloned only with their recorded consent. Guests keep their own voices or stay in the original.

Your words

Translation follows the author's script, so names, numbers and jokes come out right, not just what speech recognition heard.

Your music

The original music and ambience stay in place. Tracks are prepared to avoid Content ID problems on multi-language audio.

Long videos

Built for 30–90 minute travel and education videos, not only short clips. Non-English source languages are first-class.

How it works

  1. You upload your videos and choose the languages.
  2. We prepare the translation from your script and dub every phrase, checking each line by speech recognition.
  3. You receive the audio tracks, subtitles and translated titles, or we publish them for you as an editor on your channel.
