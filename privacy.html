Privacy Policy

EasyVideoDub is operated by MIRAMALO EOOD (UIC 207991228), 2 Veselin Hanchev St., Entrance A, Gradinski Residential Complex, 6008 Stara Zagora, Bulgaria (“we”). We process the videos, texts and voice recordings that you give us only to produce the dubbing you order. Contact: founder@easyvideodub.com. See also our Terms of Service.

What we process

  • Account: your email address and sign-in data (Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Your content: the videos you upload, their transcripts and translations, and the dubbed audio, subtitles and texts we produce.
  • Your voice (optional): if you choose to dub in your own voice, a short consent recording and a voice sample. A copy of a voice is made only for the person who recorded their own consent.
  • Payments: handled by Paddle (merchant of record). We do not see or store card details.

YouTube API Services

EasyVideoDub uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What we access: with your permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.force-ssl) we read your channel’s name and ID and your videos’ titles, thumbnails, durations and languages, and — only when you click “Publish translations” for a video — add subtitle tracks and translated titles/descriptions to that video of your own channel. They are visible on the video right away. We never upload or delete videos and never change a video’s privacy setting.
  • Video and audio: we do not download or store YouTube video or audio. Dubbing uses the video file you upload.
  • How we use it: only to show your videos in EasyVideoDub, to dub the videos you choose and to publish the results you approve to your own channel.
  • Storage: the OAuth refresh token is encrypted (Google Cloud KMS); access tokens are not stored. Video and channel details obtained from the API are kept only while needed for your projects and are deleted or refreshed at least every 30 days.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share YouTube data with third parties: not with advertisers, data brokers, lenders or anyone else. The only exceptions: the title and description of a video you ask us to translate are sent to Google’s Gemini API (paid service; Google does not use this data to train its models) to produce the translations you review; our hosting provider Google Cloud stores the data on our behalf; and we disclose data if the law requires it.
  • Revoking access: disconnect the channel in EasyVideoDub (Integrations) — we revoke our access at Google and delete the token at once — or at any time at Google security settings.

Google user data

“Google user data” means the data we receive from Google APIs: your Google account email used for sign-in and the YouTube data described above. We do not create aggregated or anonymized data sets from it.

  • Data access: your email address (sign-in); with the youtube.force-ssl permission, your channel’s name and ID and your videos’ titles, descriptions, thumbnails, durations, languages and existing subtitle tracks.
  • Data use: only to provide the features you see in EasyVideoDub: choosing a video, dubbing it and publishing the subtitles and translated titles you approve to your own channel. We never use Google user data for advertising, for credit or lending decisions, to build user profiles, or to develop, improve or train AI/ML models.
  • Data transfer: as described under “Sharing” above. Humans at EasyVideoDub read your data only with your consent (for example, a support request), for security purposes or when the law requires it.
  • Data protection: all traffic uses HTTPS (TLS); data at rest is encrypted by Google Cloud; the OAuth refresh token is additionally encrypted with Google Cloud KMS; access to production data is limited to the service accounts that need it and to the company’s administrator account.
  • Retention and deletion: API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days and deleted at once when you disconnect the channel; the OAuth token is revoked at Google and deleted at once when you disconnect; on a request to delete your account we delete your account and all its data within 30 days.

Limited Use. EasyVideoDub’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

  • Source videos are deleted after the order is delivered; finished audio tracks and subtitles are kept for at least 30 days so you can download them again, then deleted unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Your voice recordings and voice copy are deleted at once when you click “Delete my voice”; finished dubs stay.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all data: founder@easyvideodub.com.

Processors

Google Cloud (hosting, storage, Vertex AI and Gemini for speech recognition, translation and speech), Google Firebase (sign-in), Paddle (payments). We do not sell personal data and do not use your content to train AI models.

AI-generated content

Dubbed audio is generated with AI; the audio files we deliver are marked as AI-generated.
