Privacy

EasyVideoDub (operated by MIRAMALO EOOD, Bulgaria) processes the videos, scripts and voice recordings that a customer sends us only to produce the dubbing the customer ordered.

  • Voice cloning is used only for a person who has given recorded consent for their own voice.
  • Source videos are deleted after the order is delivered; finished audio tracks are kept for at least 30 days so the customer can download them again, then deleted unless the customer asks us to keep them.
  • We do not sell or share customer content. Processing uses cloud providers (Google Cloud) and our own servers.
  • Questions or deletion requests: founder@easyvideodub.com.
